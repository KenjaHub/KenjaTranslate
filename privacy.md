# KenjaTranslate Privacy Policy

**Effective date** [Enter the official release date]

**App operator** [Enter the full legal name of the App Store developer]

This policy applies to the KenjaTranslate iOS app. KenjaTranslate transcribes speech from audio playing on your device and provides on-device translation and optional cloud translation. You can use the app without creating a KenjaTranslate account.

## 1. Screen Sharing and On-Device Translation

When you start translating, the app uses an iOS system prompt to request permission to share your screen. With your permission, the app reads playback audio from the shared content, recognizes speech on your device, and displays the original transcript and on-device translation. The app reads only the audio from the shared stream. It does not read screen images or microphone audio, or save or upload the original playback audio. The first time you use certain languages, iOS may connect to the internet to download resources for speech recognition or translation.

On-Device AI is the default mode. The app processes transcripts and translations on your device without sending them to a server.

## 2. Optional Cloud AI PRO Translation

When you choose Cloud AI PRO and confirm that you want to start cloud translation, the app uses Apple Intelligence's Private Cloud Compute to translate captions. Each request includes the current finalized transcript segment, the last 1,000 characters at most from up to two preceding segments combined for context, and the target language. The transcript may contain personal information from the content you play. Cloud requests contain only the text and instructions needed for translation.

Apple's Private Cloud Compute processes this data solely to fulfill the request and securely returns the result to your device. According to Apple, Private Cloud Compute does not retain request data or results, and Apple cannot access the data it processes. The app saves the resulting cloud translation in your on-device history. [Apple Intelligence and Privacy](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iphe3f499e0e/ios)

## 3. On-Device History and Settings

The app automatically saves finalized transcripts, on-device and cloud translations, session start times, caption timing relative to the session, and the selected languages. You can review this information by session in History. The app also stores preferences such as caption styling on your device. The app does not automatically delete this on-device data after a set period. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, the backup may include app data and restore it when you restore the device from that backup.

## 4. Subscriptions and Free Usage Allowances

The App Store handles subscription purchases, payments, and purchase restoration. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to read and verify transaction information and subscription entitlements for KenjaTranslate so it can determine which features are available. It stores the verified date you first acquired the app and the time you used your weekly free session on your device to calculate the free trial period and weekly allowance. The app cannot access your payment card number or full payment details. [App Store and Privacy](https://www.apple.com.cn/legal/privacy/data/zh-cn/app-store/)

## 5. Data Sharing and Protection

The app uses Apple services for cloud translation and App Store transactions as described above. When the app shares personal information with a third party, we require the recipient to provide the same level of privacy protection as this policy and the applicable App Store Review Guidelines. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising tracking. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com.cn/legal/privacy/)

## 6. Stopping Processing, Accessing Data, and Deleting Data

- **Stop audio and cloud processing.** Stop translation in the app or stop screen sharing in iOS to end the current session. For future sessions, choose On-Device AI or decline the cloud translation prompt to avoid sending new cloud requests.
- **Access and delete on-device data.** You can view transcripts and translations in History. To remove app data from your device, including history, preferences, and local usage records, use Delete App in iOS. You can manage existing backups separately using your device's backup settings.
- **Manage your subscription.** You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS under Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. To access or submit a request about account or transaction data held by Apple, visit [Apple Data and Privacy](https://privacy.apple.com/).

You can also use the contact details below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. We use the contact details and related information you provide to respond to your request. We retain this information for as long as necessary to handle the request, or for any longer period required by applicable law.

## 7. Policy Updates and Contact Information

When we update this policy, we will post the revised policy and its effective date on this page. If you have questions about this policy or how we process personal information, please contact the app operator.

**Privacy contact email** support@kofukuai.com

# 贤者翻译（KenjaTranslate）隐私政策

**生效日期**　【填写正式发布日期】

**应用运营者**　【填写 App Store 开发者主体全称】

本政策适用于贤者翻译 iOS 应用。贤者翻译可将设备播放音频中的语音转写为字幕，并提供端侧翻译和可选的云端翻译。使用应用无需注册贤者翻译账号。

## 1. 屏幕共享与端侧翻译

开始翻译时，应用会通过 iOS 系统面板请求屏幕共享授权。经你授权后，应用从共享内容中读取设备播放音频，在设备上进行语音识别，并显示原文及端侧译文。应用仅读取共享流中的音频，不读取屏幕画面或麦克风音频，也不保存或上传原始播放音频。首次使用某些语言时，系统可能联网下载语音识别或翻译所需的语言资源。

「端侧 AI」是默认模式。字幕原文和端侧译文在设备上处理，应用不会将它们发送到服务器以完成端侧翻译。

## 2. 可选的云端AI PRO 翻译

当你选择「云端AI PRO」并确认开始云端翻译后，应用会通过 Apple Intelligence 的私有云计算（Private Cloud Compute）处理字幕，以生成云端译文。请求包含当前已定稿的原文、作为上下文的前面最多两段原文（合计最多发送末尾 1,000 个字符），以及目标语言。原文可能包含播放内容中出现的个人信息。云端请求仅发送翻译所需的文本和指令。

Apple 的私有云计算仅为完成该次请求处理这些数据，并将结果安全返回设备。根据 Apple 的说明，私有云计算不会保留请求数据或结果，Apple 也无法访问其中处理的数据。应用会将收到的云端译文写入本机历史记录。[Apple Intelligence 与隐私](https://support.apple.com/en-euro/guide/iphone/iphe3f499e0e/ios)

## 3. 本机历史记录与设置

应用会按会话自动保存定稿原文、收到的端侧及云端译文、会话开始时间、字幕相对时间和所选语言，用于在「历史记录」中回看。应用也在本机保存字幕样式等偏好设置。应用没有为这些本机数据设置自动清理期限。若开启 iCloud 备份或使用电脑备份，应用数据可能随设备备份保存，并在恢复设备备份时重新出现。

## 4. 订阅与免费使用额度

Apple App Store 负责订阅购买、付款和恢复购买。应用通过 Apple 的 StoreKit 读取并验证本应用的交易信息及订阅权益，用于确认哪些功能可用。应用在设备上缓存经验证的首次获取应用日期和每周免费会话的使用时间，用于计算免费试用期和每周额度。应用无法取得你的银行卡号或完整支付资料。[App Store 与隐私](https://www.apple.com.cn/legal/privacy/data/zh-cn/app-store/)

## 5. 数据共享与保护

应用使用 Apple 的服务完成上述云端翻译和 App Store 交易。对应用向第三方提供的个人信息，我们要求接收方提供与本政策及适用的 App Store 审核指南同等的隐私保护。应用未接入第三方广告或分析 SDK，我们不会出售个人信息，或将个人信息用于广告追踪。[Apple 客户隐私政策](https://www.apple.com.cn/legal/privacy/)

## 6. 停止处理、查看和删除数据

- **停止音频和云端处理。** 你可以在应用内停止翻译，或从 iOS 停止屏幕共享，以结束当前会话。后续可选择「端侧 AI」，或取消云端翻译确认，避免发送新的云端翻译请求。
- **查看和删除本机数据。** 你可以在「历史记录」中查看字幕和译文。你可以通过 iOS 的「删除 App」移除设备上的应用数据，包括历史记录、偏好设置和本机额度记录。你可以通过设备的备份管理功能另行管理已有备份。
- **管理订阅。** 你可以在 iOS「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中管理或取消订阅。Apple 持有的账户及交易资料可通过 [Apple 数据与隐私](https://privacy.apple.com/) 查询或申请处理。

你也可以通过下方联系方式提出访问、更正或删除个人信息的请求。为回复你的请求，我们会使用你主动提供的联系方式和相关信息，并在处理请求所需的期间内保留这些信息。适用法律要求继续保留的，按法定期间保留。
